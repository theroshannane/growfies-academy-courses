Yes! The undergraduate curriculum is delivered in partnership with UGC-recognized and accredited university partners, ensuring your bachelor's degree is fully valid for higher studies (like MBA in India or MS abroad) as well as government examinations.

Yes! You attend live, interactive virtual lectures with your professors and batchmates. There are zero mandatory physical campus commutes, allowing students from across India and worldwide to earn a world-class degree from the comfort of their home.

Candidates must have completed their 12th Standard (HSC / CBSE / ICSE or equivalent) in any stream (Commerce, Science, or Arts) with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks. Selection is based on an online aptitude test and personal interview.
