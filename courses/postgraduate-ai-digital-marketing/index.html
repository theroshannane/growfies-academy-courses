Admissions FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the eligibility for this PG Certificate Program? ▼
Candidates must hold a graduation degree from any recognised university with a minimum of 50% marks. 1+ year of work experience is preferred, though exceptional fresh graduates from BBA, B.Com, BMM, BMS, and engineering backgrounds are encouraged to apply.
What does the 45-minute qualifying selection test involve? ▼
The selection process consists of a 45-minute online multiple-choice test evaluating basic logical reasoning, communication aptitude, and general awareness of digital trends. It is non-technical, and no programming knowledge is required.
What is the schedule of the live classes? ▼
Classes are held live on weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) for 3 to 4 hours per day, accommodating working professionals and college students. All live sessions are recorded and made available within 2 hours with step-by-step notes.
Can I attend the optional 2-day campus immersion? ▼
Yes! While the core program is 100% online live, students have the option to attend an intensive 2-day in-person campus immersion workshop at our Thane/Mumbai center (Dev Corpora, Cadbury Junction, Thane W) for hands-on networking and portfolio reviews.
